# Ajo: product
Register: product (dense back-office tool plus one mobile trust page), not marketing.
Users: cooperative treasurers, officers and committee members on laptops (often modest hardware, slow networks); members on Android phones via a confirmation page.
Purpose: a trustworthy cooperative ledger that prevents unauthorized loans and deductions.
Feeling: calm, exact, accountable. Like a well-kept ledger book, not a flashy fintech app.
Never: hype, gamification, dark patterns, jargon, anything that makes money look casual.
Language: Nigerian/British English (instalment, cheque), plain words, sentence case.
